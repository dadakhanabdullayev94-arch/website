VII.
FAQ

Questions buyers ask before they click.

Will these methods work in my climate?—
Yes. The methods are written for ordinary North American homes — cold winters, hot summers, dry plains, humid coasts. Each section notes which fixes belong first depending on whether your biggest bill is winter heat, summer cooling, or year-round water. The cheap fixes work the same in every climate; the larger projects include the adjustments that matter.
I'm not handy. Is this still useful for me?+
Plenty of it needs no tools at all — the pantry, cleaning, habits, and remedies sections are mostly knowledge, not carpentry. Of the 98 methods, 44 cost $20 or less to try, and many of those are things like sealing a draft or hanging a clothesline that anyone can do in an afternoon.
How much will I actually save?+
Honestly: it depends on your house, your climate, and which methods you use, so we don't promise a figure. What we can point to is published research — the EPA puts sealing-and-insulating at about 15% off heating and cooling, and independent studies support real savings on line-drying and a home garden. See "The Math" above for the sourced numbers. We'd rather show you those than invent a headline figure.
I rent. Can I still use this?+
Much of it, yes — anything that isn't a permanent structural change. Draft sealing (the removable kind), window quilts, the clothesline, the whole pantry and kitchen and cleaning and habits sections, and container gardening all work in a rental. The book flags which methods need an owner's say-so.

It's a PDF, delivered to your email within about a minute of purchase, readable on any device. If it isn't right for you, email within 7 days and we'll refund it — you keep nothing you didn't pay for, and neither do we.
